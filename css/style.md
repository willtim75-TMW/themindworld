# The Mind World Style Guide

## Brand Overview
- **Name**: The Mind World
- **Tagline**: Enterprise Power for Everyday Entrepreneurs
- **Description**: Simple AI automation, modern websites, database marketing, social strategies for local SMBs (retail, restaurants, contracting, lawyers, doctors). Bring big-tech results without headaches.
- **Voice**: Professional, approachable, expert yet down-to-earth. Trust, simplicity, results. Speak to technical + non-technical audiences.
- **Values**: Accessibility of AI, local focus, practical innovation.

## Design Tokens

### Colors (Dark Theme, High Contrast)
- Primary: `#042d4a` (deep blue)
- Secondary/Accent: `#78a6bd` (neon blue)
- Accent Highlight: Red-orange (~#c15c2e or brain tones 120-200R)
- BG: `#021021` to `#042d4a` gradient + subtle circuit overlay
- Text Primary: `#ffffff` / light grays
- Text Secondary: `#78a6bd`

### Typography
- Headings: Modern sans-serif (system-ui/Inter)
- Body: 16px base, 1.5-1.6 line-height
- Futuristic neon glow on hero/logo text
